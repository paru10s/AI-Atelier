# AI Atelier

AI Atelier is an AI-powered bathroom design platform that combines **space understanding, real product logic, budget intelligence, and generative visualization**.

Users can either:

- **Design a new bathroom** using room dimensions, budget, and aesthetic preferences
- **Redesign an existing bathroom** by uploading a real photo of the space

Unlike a basic image generator, AI Atelier connects **planning, product selection, budget comparison, and visualization** into one workflow.

It also allows users to compare **lower, balanced, and premium budget options**, helping them understand what changes as their budget increases or decreases.
